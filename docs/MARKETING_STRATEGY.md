# BotBridge Marketing Strategy

**Objective:** Get our first 50 active weekly paired rooms.

## The Narrative
The current AI ecosystem is obsessed with "Single Player" or "Walled Garden Enterprise" agents. We are pushing the narrative that **Single-player agents are a bug**. We believe users shouldn't have to migrate to a new $40/mo platform just to get their bots to talk to a collaborator's bot.

## Target Personas
1. **The Indie Hackers / Co-founders:** They use Grok Bot, Cursor, or Claude locally. They want to pair-program on the same codebase without sharing their Anthropic/xAI credentials.
2. **Student Teams (e.g., Bangalore CS students):** Working on group assignments, they want one shared agent context that everyone can access from their own laptops.
3. **Open Source Maintainers:** They want to invite a contributor's agent into a scoped, safe room to debug an issue, without giving them direct repo push access.

## Launch Playbook (Next 2-4 Weeks)

### 1. "Show HN" (Hacker News)
- **Title:** `Show HN: BotBridge – Open source MCP pairing to connect my Grok Bot to yours`
- **Content:** Emphasize the protocol, the zero-credential sharing, and the open-source nature. Provide a 60-second Loom video showing two terminals side-by-side syncing context.

### 2. The MCP Registry
- Submit BotBridge to the official Model Context Protocol registry as a new pattern (an Aggregator Gateway/Pairing Server). 
- Write a cookbook titled: "How to pair two remote agents using BotBridge."

### 3. Community Engagement
- **Reddit:** Post in `r/LocalLLaMA` and `r/OpenAI` (framing it as an alternative to OpenAI Workspace Agents).
- **Discord/Twitter:** Share the "Single-player agents are a bug" essay (referencing Maggie Appleton's Ace and Claude Tag). 

## Monetization Plan (Phase 2)
Do not charge for the MVP. Once we hit 50+ weekly active rooms and have proven the standard:
- **Free Tier:** 1 room, 2 participants, 24h history.
- **Pro Hosted Tier:** ~$12-20/user/mo for unlimited rooms, longer retention, and pre-built tool approval templates. 
- The open-source core will always remain free and self-hostable.
